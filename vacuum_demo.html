Where readings come from
The ESP32 prints its address in the Serial Monitor after it joins Wi-Fi. The laptop must be on the same network.
Out of bounds
An event starts when vacuum strength stays below this value. It applies to new readings; existing events are kept.
Higher values stop brief noise from creating many small events.
Avoids a false event at start-up, while the pump is still pulling down.
Display
Older readings are dropped. Events and notes are kept.
