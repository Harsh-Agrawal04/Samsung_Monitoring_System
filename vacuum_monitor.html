Bound lines
Drag to pan. Scroll or pinch-scroll to zoom at the cursor. Click a point to pin its exact reading (up to 6). Arrow keys pan, + and − zoom, Esc clears pins. Pause view locks the graph to the dates you typed: you can still zoom in and pan, but only inside those dates. Unpaused, you can pan to any date in the 45-day history. Higher pressure is toward the top, so out-of-bounds excursions rise above the bound lines. The axis fits the data on screen. A bound line that is far from the data stays off the chart and is noted at the edge, so there is no empty space; it comes into view when readings approach it. Use Full sensor range to see the whole sensor span up to atmosphere (100,000 Pa).
